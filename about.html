A small, local company in Kathmandu

Beyond Summit Expeditions is run by Elwin Tamang from Thamel. We keep trips small and unhurried, and plan each one around the people on it.

Our story

Beyond Summit Expeditions was founded by Elwin Tamang to run treks and climbs where every trip is built around the people on it, and where safety comes before any summit.

Today we run treks and climbs across Nepal, from a first week in the Annapurna foothills to the high passes of the Manaslu Circuit, all planned from Kathmandu.

We are small on purpose. When you write to us, you hear back from Elwin, the person who will plan your trip.

What we stand for

Safety before summits

Turnaround times are fixed before summit day, and the guide's call is final. We have turned back many times and will again.

Small groups

Never more than eight people, so the pace suits you and nobody gets lost in the crowd.

Fair to our team

Porters are paid fairly and insured, loads are kept under 20 kg, and every porter has proper boots and a jacket.

Licensed and accountable

We're registered with Nepal's Department of Tourism, and every guide we work with holds a government trekking or climbing licence.

Leave no trace

We carry rubbish out, use boiled and filtered water instead of plastic bottles, and buy food from the villages we stay in.

Honest advice

If a trip isn't right for your experience or the season, we'll tell you, and suggest one that is.

Licences and memberships

We are a licensed trekking and mountaineering agency in Nepal. Restricted-area and climbing permits can only be issued through agencies like ours, which is one reason it matters who you book with.
