About this trek

Langtang is a short trek into a glacier valley, a day's drive north of Kathmandu. The trail climbs through forest and Tamang villages to yak pastures and the monastery at Kyanjin Gompa. Langtang village was rebuilt after the 2015 earthquake and families here rely on trekkers, so your visit genuinely helps. No flights are needed, and it fits neatly into ten days.

  • Sunrise from Tserko Ri, 4,984 m
  • Kyanjin Gompa and its cheese factory
  • Tamang villages and the rebuilt Langtang village
  • Langtang Lirung, 7,227 m, above the valley
  • No domestic flights
  • The best trek for a short trip

  • Langtang National Park entry
    NPR 3,000 per person (foreign nationals)
  • TIMS card
    Required

Both are included in our price.

Altitude and safety

Above 3,000 m we check everyone's oxygen level and pulse each evening, and talk through how you're sleeping and eating. If altitude symptoms don't ease with rest, we go down. No summit or pass is worth pushing on for. Your insurance needs to cover helicopter rescue up to 4,984 m.

Where you stay

Simple, family-run tea houses: twin rooms, shared bathrooms higher up, and a warm dining room around the stove where everyone gathers in the evening. Hot showers and phone charging cost a few dollars extra.

Fitness and preparation

If you can walk for six hours on hilly ground several days in a row, you'll be fine. Two or three months of regular hill walks with a loaded daypack is the best preparation.

Common questions

Is Langtang safe after the 2015 earthquake?

Yes. The trail and lodges were rebuilt, and Langtang village was re-established a short way from the old site. As anywhere in the mountains, landslides can block the road in the monsoon, which is why we keep a spare day.

Is Langtang good for beginners?

It is one of the best choices for a first trek. The days are moderate and you can turn round at Kyanjin Gompa (3,870 m) without climbing Tserko Ri.

Can I combine Langtang with Gosaikunda?

Yes. Crossing to the holy lakes at Gosaikunda and over the Lauribina La adds four or five days.
