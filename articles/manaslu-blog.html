About this trek

This trek circles Manaslu, the world's eighth-highest mountain, through a restricted area that only opened to trekkers in 1991. You follow the Budhi Gandaki from deep subtropical gorges up into the Tibetan Buddhist valley of Nubri, then cross the Larkya La to the Annapurna side. It's quieter than Everest or Annapurna and the lodges are simpler, which is a big part of its charm.

  • The Larkya La pass, 5,106 m, with glaciers all around
  • Samagaon and the Tibetan Buddhist villages of Nubri
  • Close views of Manaslu, 8,163 m
  • The deep Budhi Gandaki gorge
  • Far fewer trekkers than the Everest or Annapurna trails
  • Two acclimatisation days before the pass

Permits and practical details

Permits you need

  • Manaslu Restricted Area Permit
    US$100 for the first 7 days (Sep to Nov), US$75 (Dec to Aug), plus a daily fee after that
  • Manaslu Conservation Area Permit (MCAP)
    NPR 3,000 per person
  • Annapurna Conservation Area Permit (ACAP)
    NPR 3,000, for the descent through the Annapurna area

Restricted-area permits are only issued through a registered agency to trekkers walking with a licensed guide. Since March 2026 they can be issued to a single trekker. All fees are included in our price.

Altitude and safety

Above 3,000 m we check everyone's oxygen level and pulse each evening, and talk through how you're sleeping and eating. If altitude symptoms don't ease with rest, we go down. No summit or pass is worth pushing on for. Your insurance needs to cover helicopter rescue up to 5,106 m.

Where you stay

Simple, family-run tea houses: twin rooms, shared bathrooms higher up, and a warm dining room around the stove where everyone gathers in the evening. Hot showers and phone charging cost a few dollars extra.

Fitness and preparation

If you can walk for six hours on hilly ground several days in a row, you'll be fine. Two or three months of regular hill walks with a loaded daypack is the best preparation.

Common questions

Can I trek Manaslu on my own?

No. Manaslu is a restricted area: the permit is only issued through a registered agency and you must walk with a licensed guide. Since March 2026 the permit can be issued to a solo trekker, so you no longer need a second person.

How does Manaslu compare to the Annapurna Circuit?

It is quieter, more remote and a little more demanding, with longer days and simpler lodges. The scenery in the upper valley is as good as anything in Nepal.

Can I add the Tsum Valley?

Yes. Tsum branches off near Deng and adds about six days. It needs its own restricted-area permit, which we arrange.

Is the Larkya La dangerous?

It is a long, high day, sometimes on snow. We start before dawn, carry microspikes in the shoulder seasons and only cross when the weather is settled.
