About this climb

Ama Dablam is one of the most recognisable mountains in the world, and its Southwest Ridge is a real alpine climb: rock pitches, the Yellow Tower, an exposed snow ridge and the Dablam ice face, all on fixed ropes. It's for climbers who have already been above 6,000 m and feel at home on steep rock and ice in crampons.

  • The Southwest Ridge, one of the great Himalayan climbs
  • Rock, mixed and ice climbing on fixed lines
  • Base camp in a grassy meadow below the mountain
  • Personal climbing Sherpa on summit day
  • Proper rotation and rest schedule
  • Weather days built into the plan

What's included

  • Airport pick-up and drop-off in Kathmandu
  • A licensed climbing guide (1 guide to 2 climbers on summit day) and a trekking guide
  • Porters on the approach, paid and insured
  • Climbing royalty, trekking permits and conservation fees
  • Tea houses on the approach; tents, cook and meals at base and high camp
  • Group climbing gear: fixed rope, anchors, ice screws

Not included

  • Personal climbing gear (can be hired in Kathmandu or Chhukung)
  • Summit bonus and tips for guides and porters
  • Drinks, showers and charging in tea houses

All royalties and fees for your season are included.

Altitude and safety

Above 3,000 m we check everyone's oxygen level and pulse each evening, and talk through how you're sleeping and eating. If altitude symptoms don't ease with rest, we go down. No summit or pass is worth pushing on for. Your insurance needs to cover helicopter rescue up to 6,812 m.

Where you stay

Tea houses on the walk in, then two-person tents at base camp and high camp. At base camp a cook team looks after you, with a dining tent and a toilet tent.

Fitness and preparation

You need good hill fitness, some experience walking in crampons and a head for heights. When you book, we send a training plan and a full kit list, and we check your gear together in Kathmandu.

Common questions

What experience do I need for Ama Dablam?

At least one previous climb above 6,000 m (Island Peak, Lobuche East or Mera are typical), and confidence moving on rock and steep ice in crampons with a jumar. We ask for a climbing CV and may suggest a warm-up peak first.

Is supplemental oxygen used?

Most climbers summit Ama Dablam without oxygen. We carry oxygen for medical use.

Spring or autumn?

Autumn (October to November) is the main season, with stable weather and good snow conditions. Spring is possible but has more cloud.
