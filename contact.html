Quick Answers

Frequently Asked

How far in advance should I book?
For Everest and other 8000m peaks, we recommend booking 12–18 months ahead. Permits are limited and slots fill early. Treks can often be arranged with 4–6 weeks notice.
What experience level do I need?
It varies by programme. EBC treks require good fitness but no technical skill. Ama Dablam needs solid mountaineering experience. Everest requires multiple 6000m+ summits. We assess every applicant individually.
Is travel insurance required?
Yes, comprehensive travel and evacuation insurance is mandatory for all expeditions. We can recommend specialist providers that cover high-altitude climbing and helicopter evacuation.
What is included in the expedition price?
All permits, Sherpa wages, O₂ (for high-altitude programmes), base camp meals, tents, and logistics. International flights, personal equipment, and insurance are arranged by you. Full inclusions are listed on each programme page.
Can I do a solo private expedition?
Absolutely. Many of our clients prefer a fully private programme with a dedicated Sherpa team. We offer bespoke solo and small-group packages — contact us to discuss your requirements.
How do I pay and what is the deposit?
A 30% deposit confirms your place. The balance is due 60 days before departure. We accept bank transfer and major cards. A detailed payment schedule is provided in your booking agreement.
