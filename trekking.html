Planning Your Trip

Best Seasons to
Trek in Nepal

🌸
Spring
March – May
Rhododendrons in bloom, stable weather, excellent visibility. Peak season for Everest region and Annapurna.
🌧️
Monsoon
June – August
Heavy rain in most regions. Upper Mustang lies in Annapurna's rain shadow — ideal for Mustang trekking.
🍂
Autumn
September – November
Crystal-clear post-monsoon skies, perfect temperature, stunning photography. Our favourite season overall.
❄️
Winter
December – February
Cold but tranquil. Lower regions remain open. Experienced trekkers enjoy the solitude and snow-draped peaks.
