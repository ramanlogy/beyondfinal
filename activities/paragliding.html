Paragliding in Pokhara

Pokhara is one of the best places in the world to fly. You launch from Sarangkot (about 1,600 m), ride the thermals above Phewa Lake with the Annapurna range and Machapuchare in front of you, and land on the lakeshore. Tandem flights need no experience.

Common questions

Is paragliding in Pokhara safe?

Pokhara has some of the most reliable flying conditions in Nepal. We only book licensed tandem pilots from established companies, and flights don't go up in poor weather.

When is the best time to fly?

October to May. Flights still run in the monsoon on clear days, but cancellations are more common.

Add paragliding to your trip

Most people add it before or after a trek. Tell us your dates and we'll fit it in.
