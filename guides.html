Our team

Beyond Summit Expeditions is a one-person company: Elwin Tamang plans your trip, answers your messages and leads on the trail.

Founder and lead guide

Elwin Tamang

Elwin Tamang founded Beyond Summit Expeditions and runs it from Thamel, Kathmandu.

Elwin plans every trip personally, from the first message about dates to the drive back to your hotel, and leads groups on the trail. The photos on this site are from those trips.

When you write to us, Elwin is the one who answers.

What that means for you

You deal with one person from start to finish, so nothing gets lost between an office and the trail. If you'd like to talk before you book, we'll set up a video call.

  • One point of contactThe person you message is the person who plans your trip.
  • Small groups onlyNever more than eight trekkers, so everyone gets attention.
  • Porters treated fairlyPaid fairly, insured, and loads kept under 20 kg.
