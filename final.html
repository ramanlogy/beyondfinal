Our Story

Born from the
Mountains

Fifteen years of ascents, discoveries, and lives transformed at altitude. A Kathmandu-rooted company with a single obsession: taking you higher, safely.

Who We Are

We are a team of Sherpa climbers, mountain guides, and altitude believers — built in Thamel, hardened on Everest.

Beyond Summit Expeditions was founded in 2010 by Dawa Sherpa and Pemba Norbu, two Everest summiteers who believed that high-altitude mountaineering could be safer, more personalised, and more deeply connected to Nepal's Sherpa heritage than existing operators offered.

From our first season with 6 clients on Ama Dablam, we have grown into Nepal's most trusted expedition operator — running programmes across all fourteen 8000-metre peaks, countless technical routes, and Nepal's most iconic trekking corridors.

We are licensed by the Department of Tourism, Government of Nepal. Our Sherpa guides hold UIAA and IFMGA certification. And every single member of our logistics team has been on the mountain — not just in an office.

Our mission has never changed: to design expeditions where your summit becomes possible because everything else is impossible to fail.

Our Journey

Fifteen Years of
Ascent

2010
Founded in Thamel
Dawa Sherpa and Pemba Norbu establish Beyond Summit Expeditions. First season: six climbers, one mountain (Ama Dablam), six summits. A 100% success rate from day one.
2013
First Everest Season
We run our inaugural Everest expedition. Four of five clients summit. Our first O₂ and high-altitude medical protocols are developed, later adopted as industry standards.
2018
500th Summit Milestone
We reach 500 successful summits across all programmes. Launch of our Trekking Division, opening Nepal's classic routes to a new audience with the same attention to detail.
2022
All 14 Eight-Thousanders
Beyond Summit becomes one of only three Nepal operators to offer guided programmes on all fourteen 8000m peaks simultaneously. 1,800 total summits and counting.
2026
Spring Season Opens
2,400+ summits achieved. Spring 2026 is our largest season yet — seven concurrent expedition teams across five peaks. The story continues. Yours can begin now.
What We Stand For

Core
Values

Safety Above All
No summit is worth a life. We have walked away from the top — and we are proud of it. Strict turnaround times, real-time medical monitoring, weather holds honoured without debate.
Sherpa Heritage
Our company is Sherpa-founded and Sherpa-led. We invest in our guide teams, pay above-industry wages, and share expedition profits with the communities that make our work possible.
Personalised Experience
Maximum 8 climbers per team. Your Sherpa guide is assigned to you personally. We know your name, your training history, and your goals before you land in Kathmandu.
Relentless Preparation
Months of planning behind every expedition. Logistics, permits, equipment, medical supplies, acclimatisation planning — all meticulously arranged so you can focus on climbing.
Transparent Pricing
What you see is what you pay. Full permit fees, Sherpa wages, oxygen, meals and accommodation at Base Camp — all included, all disclosed. No hidden costs. No surprises.
Environmental Stewardship
Leave no trace on every route. We fund annual clean-up expeditions on Everest and Manaslu, and source all supplies locally to support Nepali mountain communities.

The Sherpas Who
Make It Real

Not just guides. Mountaineers who have lived on these peaks, who know the weather patterns, the crevasses, and the precise moment to turn back. This is who stands between you and the summit.

Our Philosophy

A Sherpa guide is not a porter. They are mountain intellectuals — reading terrain, weather, and human physiology simultaneously.

Every guide at Beyond Summit has summited their peak at least twice before they lead a client team. They hold wilderness first-aid certification, high-altitude sickness management training, and UIAA-recognised climbing qualifications. Many have also completed the Himalayan Mountaineering Institute's guide programme. We pay our Sherpas above industry rates, provide full insurance, and co-own the company with our senior team leads.

Sherpa Way

The Mountain is
Our Teacher

"We do not conquer the mountain. We ask permission to pass through. The mountain always decides."
— Sherpa Proverb
01
Lhakpa — Patient Endurance
The Sherpa approach to altitude is never to fight it. We read the mountain's rhythm, respect acclimatisation windows, and wait when the peak demands patience over urgency.
02
Nying — Heartfelt Care
Every client we guide is extended genuine familial care. We notice when you're struggling before you do. This attentiveness has saved lives and created lifelong bonds.
03
Rigpa — Mountain Knowledge
Our guides carry knowledge of these mountains that cannot be taught in a classroom. Route conditions, wind patterns, snow stability — knowledge built across hundreds of ascents and generations.
