Jungle Safari

Nepal's wild south — the Terai — is one of Asia's most biodiverse regions. Chitwan National Park shelters the one-horned rhinoceros, Bengal tiger, gharial crocodile, and over 500 species of bird. Our guided jeep and elephant safaris take you deep into the sal forest at dawn, when the jungle awakens. This is Nepal's other side — raw, humid, and breathtaking.

Why Nepal

The World's Most Diverse Adventure Destination

In a single day you can watch the sun rise over the Himalayas, raft a Grade 4 river through the middle hills, and fall asleep listening to jungle wildlife. No other country on earth compresses this much adventure into this small a space.

Vertical Variety
From 60m above sea level in the Terai jungle to 8,848m — every adventure operates in a different world.
Year-Round Access
While the high peaks have seasonal windows, most outdoor activities run year-round with expert guides.
Local Expert Guides
Every activity is led by certified Nepali guides who know their terrain intimately and keep safety paramount.
Nepal's Adventure Record
Home to the world's highest bungee, steepest zip line, highest paragliding launch sites, and wildest whitewater — all in a country smaller than Arkansas.
