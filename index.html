How we travel

Small groups, at the pace of the mountains

We keep groups to eight people or fewer and build acclimatisation days into every itinerary. Nights are spent in family-run tea houses, and the plan bends to the weather and to how you feel, not to a fixed schedule.

  • Private departures on dates that suit you
  • Permits, flights and transfers handled before you land
  • Porters paid and insured, with fair loads

Our guides

Guides who grew up below these peaks

Our Sherpa team has more than 150 Everest summits between them. On the trail that experience shows up as small, careful decisions: when to rest, when to turn back, and when to push on.

  • Wilderness first aid certified, with daily oxygen checks
  • Satellite phone and helicopter evacuation arranged
  • One guide per climber on summit day

Every trip is built around your dates, fitness and experience. Most travellers book three to six months ahead for spring (March to May) and autumn (September to November).
