Why Beyond Summit

Your Safety.
Our Mission.

For fifteen seasons we have guided humans to the highest places on earth. Not by rushing — but by understanding the mountain, respecting its moods, and preparing with obsessive care.

Safety-First Protocol
High-altitude medics on every expedition. Daily weather briefings. Strict turnaround policies no summit justifies breaking.
UIAA-Certified Sherpa Guides
Combined 150+ Everest summits across our team. Their knowledge of these mountains exists nowhere else on earth.
Small, Focused Groups
Max 8 climbers per team. More attention per person, better acclimatisation management, safer operations for all.
Premium Logistics
Heated base camp, Poisk O₂ systems, satellite communications, helicopter evacuation on standby throughout.
Sherpa-to-Climber Ratio "The mountains are not stadiums where I satisfy my ambition to achieve. They are cathedrals where I practice my religion."

Summit
