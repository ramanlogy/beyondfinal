When to go

Spring and autumn are the main seasons, but there's somewhere good to walk in every month of the year.

Spring

March to May

Rhododendrons in flower and warm days on the trail. The views get hazier towards late May.

Monsoon

June to August

Wet and cloudy on most trails, with one great exception: Upper Mustang, which stays dry.

Autumn

September to November

Crisp, clear skies after the rains. October is the classic month, and the busiest.

Winter

December to February

Cold nights, and snow often closes the high passes. Lower treks like Poon Hill are quiet and beautiful.
